# F1 Predictive Analysis

This context describes race-strategy concepts used by the analysis pipelines and dashboard.

## Language

**Strategy Attempt**:
A rival-focused pit strategy decision that can be labeled as successful or unsuccessful after a later evaluation lap. Undercut and overcut attempts are both strategy attempts.
_Avoid_: Strategy event, attack record

**Undercut**:
A strategy attempt where a driver pits before the direct rival ahead, trying to gain track position after both cars have stopped.
_Avoid_: Early stop attack

**Overcut**:
A strategy attempt where a driver stays out after the direct rival ahead pits, trying to gain track position after both cars have stopped.
_Avoid_: Stay-out attack
